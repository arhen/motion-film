# Kinds of motion video

"Make a video" covers very different jobs. Name the kind before anything else: it sets the length, the format, where the voice comes from, the story shape and what you need from the owner.

## When the request is unclear

If the request names no kind, or two kinds fit, offer a menu instead of guessing. Use your harness's question tool if it has one; otherwise, one message.

- Pick the **three likeliest kinds** from what you read during intake, one line each, with your recommendation first.
- Add "something else" as the last option.
- Ask the voice source and format in the same message (see `SKILL.md` §2), so the owner answers once.

Example:

> Which of these is closest?
> 1. **Landing-page loop** (recommended): 18 s, muted autoplay, captions carry the story.
> 2. **Feature showcase**: 30 s, one flow from the app, rebuilt from its components.
> 3. **Talking-head clip**: your recording cut to 30 s for Reels, with diagrams beside you.
> 4. Something else.

## The menu

### Product

| Kind | What it is | Length | Formats | Voice | Music | You need |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Landing-page loop** | A muted, seamless explainer above the fold | 15-22 s | 16:9 | optional; captions carry it | synthesized, quiet | product truth, tokens |
| **Feature showcase** | One flow of the real product, rebuilt from its components | 20-45 s | 16:9, plus 9:16 cut | AI or none | synthesized | the live product, sample data |
| **Launch or marketing film** | A story-led film: a person, a problem, the turn, the result | 30-60 s | 16:9 and 9:16 | AI or own | supplied track or synthesized | positioning, brand, approvals |
| **Showreel or sizzle** | A beat-locked montage of what the product does | 20-40 s | 16:9 | none or a few lines | beat-driven | many screens |
| **App store or onboarding preview** | Device-framed walkthrough of first-run value | 15-30 s | device portrait | none | light | store specs, screens |

### Content

| Kind | What it is | Length | Formats | Voice | Music | You need |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tutorial or how-to** | Steps on a screen recording or rebuilt UI, with zooms and step chips | 30 s-3 min | 16:9; 9:16 tips | own recording or AI | soft bed | the recording, or the steps |
| **Concept or term explainer** | One idea built on screen from words and diagrams, or as a machine when the idea is a system with parts | 30-90 s | 9:16 and 16:9 | AI or own | soft bed | the definition and its source |
| **Article or post explainer** | An article's argument in three points, with the source on screen | 45-120 s | 9:16 and 16:9 | AI or own | soft bed | the article (URL or text) |
| **Talking-head clip** | Your own footage cut tight, with captions, punch-ins and visual explanations beside or over you | 15-60 s | 9:16 | the footage's own voice | light bed, ducked | the footage, rights to it |
| **Series kit** | The fixed pieces of a recurring show: intro, lower thirds, caption style, end card | 3-8 s each | as the series | none | sonic logo | channel brand |

Footage-based kinds (tutorial from a recording, talking-head clip) use `motion-film-footage` for transcription, the cut list and layouts.

## Story shapes

Each kind has a shape. The beats are a starting point; the subject decides the details.

- **Landing-page loop:** the situation in one image, the product meets it, one turn, the result, then back to the opening still. Frame 0 is readable and already moving.
- **Feature showcase:** the job someone has → the flow in three to five moves, each with a visible cause, the product's behaviour and a result held for reading → the outcome → sign-off.
- **Launch or marketing film:** situation → the product or idea meets it → one clear insight or turn → the person acts → a visible result → sign-off.
- **Showreel:** an opening hit, rising density of real screens, a short lull, the biggest moment in the last quarter, a clean sign-off.
- **Tutorial:** show the finished result first → the steps, one idea each (numbered chips are honest here, because the steps are real) → a recap → what to try next. Zoom to where the action is; return to the full screen between steps.
- **Concept or term explainer:** a hook (a question or a surprising case) → the definition in one plain sentence → a visual model or analogy that builds piece by piece (for a system, the machine assembles and runs; see "Mechanism explainer") → one concrete example → the takeaway line.
- **Article explainer:** the claim in one line → at most three key points, one visual each → so what → a source card (title, author or outlet, date, URL) held long enough to read.
- **Talking-head clip:** the strongest line first (the hook, within the first two seconds) → the point → visual support beside or over the speaker → the payoff or the call to action. One idea per clip.
- **Series kit:** the same intro, lower thirds, caption style and end card every episode, so recognition does the work.

## Honesty per kind

- **Product kinds:** people, businesses and numbers are real and approved, or fictional and labelled as sample data.
- **Article and term explainers:** every claim, number and quote matches the source, and the source appears on screen. No invented statistics.
- **Footage kinds:** the owner has the rights to the footage and the consent of the people in it. A cut never changes what the speaker meant: never splice words into a sentence they did not say, and never cut a qualifier ("not", "sometimes") out of a claim.
